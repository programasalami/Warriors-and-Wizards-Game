## 2026-09-23 - v0.3.8 - A Skill slot and a tidier options menu
- A new Skill slot sits on the left edge of the screen, right above your gear, with a faint S while it is empty. Skills are a new kind of item that will drop from monsters and chests and make your adventure easier in all sorts of ways. There are no skills in the game yet, so the slot is waiting for the first ones. Double-click a skill to put it in or take it out.
- Fixed: health and magic bars slid off to the left of their dark background as they emptied (on the training dummies, on monsters and under your own character). The coloured part now shrinks from the right, like it should.
- Fixed: the game kept starting you at whatever angle you last turned the camera to, instead of your Default Camera Angle. Every world now starts at your chosen angle, and the Reset To Default Camera Angle key goes back to it. "45" in that option now really means 45 degrees.
- The options menu: Controls is now called General and is sorted into Movement, Camera, Actions and Interface. Show Options, Switch Tabs, Escape To Nexus and both potion keys moved there. Hot Keys is now called Inventory and holds the eight inventory slot keys. Show HP/MP Bars and Allow Camera Rotation moved to the Extra tab. Your own choices and keys are kept.
- Messages such as "Update required", login errors and "Retry" now use the game's brown frames and parchment buttons instead of a plain grey box.
- The launcher has a new look: an all-black window with floating embers like the website's, and the title in the game's own lettering. It updates itself the next time you start it.
- The website has a Home tab, the browser version is called the Web Client, and the Download page has a "Having trouble with the launcher?" section with answers to common problems. The Portal got a Home button by its logo, a Wiki tab (Items and Classes) and a tidier tab row.

## 2026-09-22 - v0.3.7 - XP you can see, a smarter Portal
- XP, level-ups, fame and potion heals now float up over your head ("+70 XP", "Level 5!", "+1 Fame", "+30 HP"). They were sent all along but never shown. Several at once stack instead of piling up.
- If the servers update while you are playing, the game now tells you "Update required" as soon as you are back in the book, instead of bouncing you out of every PLAY with no reason. In the browser the prompt has a Refresh button that loads the new version.
- The Portal: Last Seen now shows when you last entered the game, and new accounts show when they were created. Accounts made before today have no creation date on record, so theirs stays empty.
- The Portal search finds more: one or two letters match names that start with them, three or more match anywhere in the name (exact name first, then names starting with it).
- The in-game Portal has an X next to the back arrow that leaves the Portal in one click.
- A brand new launcher (WaWLauncher): a real window with a progress bar, download speed, the installed and newest version, a big PLAY button, and check boxes for a desktop shortcut, a Start menu shortcut and closing the launcher when the game starts. Open game folder, Release notes and Repair are one click away.
- The game is now a single WarriorsAndWizards.exe sitting right next to the launcher, instead of hiding in a folder of 200 files. Your old install moves over by itself the next time you start it; your settings stay.
- The website puts the Windows and Linux downloads front and centre, the Download page is much shorter, and every page uses easier-to-read fonts. The browser version is still there as the "web version".

## 2026-09-22 - v0.3.6 - Release history
- The Portal has a release history now: every update, newest first, with the game version it came with. On the website it is the new Releases tab; in the game it is RELEASE HISTORY on the Portal's home page.
- The News Board in the Nexus shows each update's version next to its date.
- The Portal website has a Home link back to warriorsandwizards.com.
- The game window is called Warriors & Wizards now.
- Rocks are plain pictures again (the crossed look stays on trees, bushes and the shrub).
- Options: the Extra tab is sorted into Minimap, Camera, Display and Items sections (it scrolls), and gained Toggle Performance Stats, Bag Priority, Snap Camera Rotation, Player Position, Default Camera Angle, Toggle Player Position, Reset Map Scale, Fullscreen, Fullscreen type and Switch item to/from backpack from the other tabs. Your own choices and keys are kept.

## 2026-09-22 - v0.3.6 - A faster world
- The game draws walls, boards, trees, bushes, rocks and logs much faster: they are now built once per area instead of being rebuilt every single frame. On a test PC the part of each frame spent on them fell from about 3 milliseconds to about 0.1, and it no longer grows while you walk.
- Owners: /god makes your own character take no damage (type it again to switch it off).

## 2026-09-22 - v0.3.6 - Your settings stick
- Fixed: changing an option (VSync, keys, anything on the options pages) was only saved when the game closed normally or you pressed PLAY, so a crash or a forced close brought the old value back next time. Every change is now saved the moment you make it.

## 2026-09-22 - v0.3.5 - Steady props and an honest FPS readout
- Bushes, shrubs, rocks and trees no longer whirl on the spot when you turn the camera: each now stands in the world as a star of upright pictures (four for trees, six for bushes, a plain cross for rocks), so the camera walks round them the way it does round the Bug Board.
- Logs still lie on the ground and turn with it, but they have thickness again: the picture is stacked into a raised shape with a darker side, so a log reads as an object and not as a patch of ground.
- Your marker on the minimap is a bigger green square now instead of the blue arrow that kept changing size. The new Extra tab in the options lets you pick its shape (square, circle, diamond, triangle) and colour.
- Long entries on the News Board no longer run off the bottom of the window: a page stops at the last line that fits and the entry continues on the next page.
- Turning the camera with Q / E now starts and stops smoothly instead of jumping to full speed.
- The DEV readout (F5) tells the truth now: FPS over the last second and what is holding it there (VSync at your monitor's rate, an FPS cap, or nothing), what each frame costs on the CPU and on the GPU, how long it waits for the monitor, and the frame rate you would get with no limit at all. Draw calls and uploaded data per frame are listed too, and the numbers go to the console every 10 seconds.

## 2026-09-22 - v0.3.5 - New default keys, one placeholder picture, fixes
- Fixed: thin coloured lines under the floating orbs and squared around the crystal glows on the title screen.
- Combat pays off now: killing an enemy gives every player who hit it XP (a tenth of its health), levels come with stat growth and a full heal, and every 1,000 XP earns a point of character fame.
- The two targets in the Nexus you can destroy pay XP like any enemy and stand back up five seconds later. (The three DPS dummies cannot die.)
- Your health and magic regenerate over time: Vitality sets the health rate, Wisdom the magic rate.
- Health Potions work: double-click one, press C, or press its slot number (1-8). Shift-click still works too.
- Double-click a piece of gear in your inventory to equip it (or a worn piece to take it off).
- Enter submits the Sign In and Register forms.
- Loot bags disappear after a minute.
- The nearby-player menu sits on a parchment scroll and the name no longer runs into its close button.
- New default keys: O opens the options, F escapes to the Nexus, C drinks a health potion, Z toggles centering on your character, X resets the camera angle. Escape still closes any open window. Your own key choices are kept.
- Fixed: the Player Position key (Z) did nothing. It now switches your character between the exact middle of the screen and the lowered position that shows more ahead. The old "Center On Player" switch in the Graphics tab, which never did anything, is gone; use Player Position on the Controls tab.
- Fixed: spawning a monster with the owner's command froze the Nexus for everyone until the server was restarted. Spawned monsters now enter the world properly, and a failing timed action is dropped instead of stalling the world. The spawn command also understands "pirates", any capitalisation, display names and unique partial names, and suggests names when several match.
- Fixed: an item given to you (or picked up) could stay invisible in your inventory until you changed worlds. The inventory now redraws itself whenever it falls out of step.
- Fixed: walking looked slightly skippy in the browser, the character shifting back and forth a pixel or two. The camera is now placed after the character moves each frame, so it stays pinned.
- Fixed: Spawn Location now works in the browser too. The web client always sent you to the Nexus whatever you picked; the downloadable client was fine.
- The Warrior's armor slot is simply called Armor now (it was Heavy Armor, from a time with more classes).
- The Old Robe has its picture back (it shares the Old Armor's look on purpose).
- Fixed: on the Daily Gift page, "You got ..." and "Next gift in ..." were drawn on top of each other after opening a gift.
- Fixed: after picking a new key in the options, the key name sat off-centre in its box until the menu was reopened.
- Everything still waiting for its own art (the DPS dummies, the guild hall furniture, the portals, loot bags, projectiles) shows the same Health Potion picture for now, so it is obvious what is a stand-in.
- Fixed: a deleted character no longer takes up a slot. Delete both of yours and you get two NEW tiles back, one per free slot.
